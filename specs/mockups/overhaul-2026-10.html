Sanskritam screen mockups

Direction for the October redesign, drawn with the app's own colours. Layout and hierarchy are the point. Colour is always a deep solid fill with light text, the way the book covers use it. The guru art, the icons and the book covers are stand-ins.

Talk

  • Your sentence shows the way you said it, each language in its own script.
  • Correction and Why open in place. Nothing is removed.
  • The input bar loses the speaker and drops from about 105dp to about 60dp. The guru keeps his size.

Ask

  • The sheet slides straight up. No bounce.
  • This screen attaches what you are looking at. Its text is read on the phone.
  • A mic sits next to the box. Answers come as a title, dates, steps and terms.

Play

  • Games and practice are separate.
  • Every game has a clock, a score, a combo and a best.
  • One challenge a day keeps its own streak.

Grammar

  • Each chapter has its own colour and its own letter.
  • Tables fit the screen. An ending is coloured as a whole letter, so no dotted circles.
  • Callouts lose their boxes. The next topic waits at the end.

Read

  • Covers on a shelf, with Continue on top.
  • The page fills the screen. Tap the middle for controls, swipe or tap an edge to turn.
  • Light, sepia and dark, plus text size. The verse is printed as a verse, with its words one tap away.
