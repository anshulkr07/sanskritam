# Mudrā, previews and plans — 6 November 2026

Owner's decisions, all on 2026-11-06:
- Coins instead of single store purchases, named **Mudrā**: a gold coin with ॐ.
  The design is the **flat seal**, with ॐ set in Noto Serif Devanagari Bold.
- People buy Mudrā (packs) or get them every month with Lite or Plus. They
  spend them on books, audiobooks and Guru minutes, and later on avatars.
- Designs chosen on the canvas (https://claude.ai/artifact/N7SPhGgMgPjmM23SmpkNhN):
  - **Buy sheet B**: cover and three choices.
  - **Preview end B**: a faded page with one buy button; the owner removed its
    "watch an ad" option.
  - **Shop B**: a ledger.
- **Previews are 5%**, not a chapter:
  - Books: 5% of a book's pages, picked once, the same for everyone, mostly from
    the start with some from the middle and the end, never one continuous run.
    This is how Google Books works. A book with two chapters would otherwise
    give half of itself away.
  - Audiobooks: their first 5% of running time.
- **Plans like Claude's Pro and Max against its API**: a monthly allowance that
  costs far less than the same use bought piece by piece.
  - It includes more than "no ads and Mudrā": offline downloads, Guru minutes,
    scored recitations, and future avatars.
  - Lite and Plus get more Mudrā than before.
  - Later the same day: more Guru minutes, 30 a month on Lite and 120 on Plus
    (they were 15 and 60).

    PA.NUS.FCRF.
- Play Console retired pricing templates on 27 October 2025.
  - Prices are set per country, or in bulk with a one-time conversion that does
    not follow later exchange-rate moves.
  - Refresh them by hand once or twice a year.

## 2. Previews (built)

**Books** (`backend/app/commerce/preview.py`):
- Free pages: 5% of the pages (one verse page each), rounded up, and at least
  one.
- The opening page is always free.
- About 60% of the free pages come from the first fifth of the book. The rest
  are one each from equal stretches of the middle and the end.
- No two free pages are next to each other.
- The pick is seeded from the book's slug, so it is the same for every reader
  and every server. Changing `PICK_VERSION` redraws every book.
- The Gītā (700 pages) shows 35, eight of them in chapter 1. Five chapters have
  none.

**Audiobooks**:
- Free listening: the first 5% of total running time, and always at least the
  whole first verse. About 9 minutes of a 3-hour Gītā.
- The server still cuts the audio itself, so the app never receives a full file
  it has not paid for.

- Free pages, and between them one page for the pages that are not free (Preview
  end B). It shows a lock, "Chapter 1, pages 22 to 38", "It has 35 of the 700

  grey bars where the verse would be, never its text.

  link to skip to the next chapter that has free pages.
- The contents page: "Free preview: 35 of 700 pages", "Buy once, read all 18",

## 3. Plans (built)

| Each plan month | Free | Lite ₹99 | Plus ₹299 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mudrā | — | 150 (was 120) | 500 (was 400) |
| Guru minutes | 3 once, to try | 30 | 120 |
| Scored recitations | 2 a day | 100 | 500 |
| Books, audiobooks bought with Mudrā | list price | 25% off | 50% off |
| Ads | yes | none | none |
| Save offline | what you buy | free books and chants too | same |
| AI on a busy day | waits for the shared daily budget | never waits | never waits |
| Daily AI allowance | 1× | 2× | 3× |
| Premium avatars | buy with Mudrā | 25% off | all included |
| Worth in Mudrā | — | about 420 | about 1,580 |

How the allowances work:
- A plan month starts on the day of purchase or renewal. An annual plan has
  twelve.
- Guru minutes and recitations do not carry over to the next month. Mudrā do.
- An annual plan adds its Mudrā one month at a time, so a refunded year cannot
  have been spent in advance. A refund takes back every month already added.

**Guru minutes** are used in this order: the free trial, then the plan month's
minutes, then minutes bought with Mudrā. Unused plan time from a call goes back
to its month. Every companion uses the same minutes.

**Scored recitations**:
- Only practice recitations that got a score are counted.
- Competition entries are never counted, so plans buy no advantage in
  competitions.
- Free accounts have no daily limit until purchases are switched on, so nothing
  changes before launch.

**Premium avatars** are a rule in the plans module only. The shop hides that
row until the first premium avatar exists, because the Play listing must not
promise a feature that is not there. Today the app has one Guru: the Guru
voice screen uses the base settings in `mobile/src/voiceLab/config.ts`
(Bulbul v3, `ashutosh`, Sanskrit, pace 0.88). The October chooser of 27
companions is no longer in the app.

**Worth in Mudrā** is the plan's Mudrā plus its Guru minutes at the best
Mudrā rate (270 Mudrā for 30 minutes). Recitations are not sold for Mudrā, so
they are not counted.

**The risk, stated plainly.** A plan month with every allowance used costs us:

| Plan | Guru minutes | Recitations | Total | Received |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lite | 30 × ₹2 | 100 × ₹0.33 | ₹93 | ₹71 |
| Plus | 120 × ₹2 | 500 × ₹0.33 | ₹405 | ₹215 |

- The plan's Mudrā spent entirely on Guru adds up to ₹32 (Lite) or ₹110 (Plus).
- A member who uses everything costs more than they pay. At a quarter of the
  allowances, Plus costs about ₹100 and Lite about ₹23.
- This is the same bet Claude's plans make. The caps keep the worst case at
  about 1.9 times Plus's revenue and 1.3 times Lite's.
- The Guru minute is the lever. Sarvam's Bulbul V4 Flash voice is ₹2.00 per
  1,000 characters against v3's ₹3.00 (pricing page, read 2026-11-06). Whether
  it sounds right for Guru is the owner's call; the pipeline is `VOICE_CONFIG`
  in `hybrid.py`.

## 3a. Every price, defined (owner's request, later on 2026-11-06)

### Plans and Mudrā together, not Mudrā alone

Keep both. Mudrā is for buying things to keep and for occasional use. A plan is
for people who come back daily, which talking with avatars and groups will
encourage.

- Character.AI sells this mix. Its App Store listing, read 2026-11-06, shows
  Character.AI Lite ($2.49 and $4.99), Character.AI+ ($9.99 a month or $94.99 a
  year) and "Charms" packs from $0.99 to $99.99.
- Pocket FM, India's large paid-audio app, sells coin packs from ₹9 to ₹199
  next to "Pocket Premium" at ₹129. Its App Store India listing was read the
  same day.
- Google's fee does not favour either. Subscriptions are 15%. Other in-app
  purchases are 15% on the first $1M a year and 30% above it. Alternative
  billing in India is 4% less (Play Console help, "service fees", read
  2026-11-06).
- Without a plan, nothing removes ads, and there is no steady monthly income to
  carry the AI costs.

### Member prices

- Lite pays 25% less and Plus 50% less for books, audiobooks and premium
  avatars bought with Mudrā. These cost nothing to serve, so the discount costs
  nothing either.
- Talk minutes and recitations are not discounted. They cost real money, so
  members get them as a monthly allowance instead.
- What a member buys at the member price stays theirs after the plan ends.
- The server charges the member price (`mudra.member_price`, rounded down). The
  app shows it with the list price struck through.

### The other prices

- **Talk minutes** (Guru now; avatars later; one pool): 5 for 50, 15 for 140,
  30 for 270, with no member discount.
  - They are now sold on the Guru screen, not in the shop.
  - An avatar on a costlier voice (for example a Gemini voice) should count 2

- **Premium avatars** (next): 149 Mudrā each, kept for good. Lite pays 111.
  Plus has every premium avatar while subscribed.
- **Groups** (next): free to create and join; they bring people in and cost
  nothing. An avatar inside a group call listens to several people, so it
  should use the host's minutes at 2×.
- **Mudrā packs**: 14 store prices, from ₹49 (50 Mudrā) to ₹4,999 (6,000).
  - One Mudrā a rupee. Packs from ₹299 get 10% extra, and from ₹999 20%
    (`hybrid.mudra_for_inr`).
  - Google Play sells only fixed prices, so "Custom amount" steps through these.
  - The 100, 330 and 1,200 packs are also shown as rows.
- **Plans**: the numbers do not change. The discounts add value at no AI cost.
  - Lite's 150 Mudrā buy one long book a month at Lite's price (149).
  - Plus's 500 buy two long books with their audiobooks at Plus's price
    (2 × 200), with 100 to spare.

### Texts whose licence forbids commercial use

The Garuḍa Purāṇa, Arthaśāstra and Līlāvatī come from GRETIL under CC BY-NC-SA
4.0. That licence allows only uses "not primarily intended for or directed
towards commercial advantage or monetary compensation" (s.1), and ads inside the
text are monetary compensation.

- They are never sold and get no ad pages or player ads. Everyone can save them
  offline; they are not a plan perk. This is `commerce/licensing.py`, read from
  each book's recorded licence.
- The Digital Corpus of Sanskrit (CC BY 4.0) has the Arthaśāstra and the
  Garuḍapurāṇa (its GitHub file list, read 2026-11-06). Importing those two from
  there would make them ordinary books. Līlāvatī is not in it.
- CC BY and CC BY-SA texts (Digital Corpus, sa.wikisource) may be sold, with
  their credits shown. Their 4.0 licences forbid applying technological
  measures that restrict what recipients may do with the text (s.2(a)(5)(C)),
  so offline copies of these texts must not be encrypted.

## 5. Owner steps

1. **Play Console**:
   - Create the 14 consumable Mudrā packs, named by the Mudrā they give:
     `com.sanskritam.app.mudra.50`, `.150`, `.200`, `.250`, `.100`, `.330`,
     `.440`, `.550`, `.770`, `.1200`, `.1800`, `.2400`, `.3600`, `.6000`. Their

     phone are not charged.
   - Create subscriptions `lite.v2.monthly`, `lite.v2.annual`,
     `plus.v2.monthly` and `plus.v2.annual`.
   - Set prices from the table in section 1, country by country or in bulk.
2. **Backend**:
   - Run migrations `20261104_hybrid_commerce` and `20261106_plan_allowances`.
   - Set `COMMERCE_ENABLED`, `COMMERCE_HYBRID_ENABLED` and
     `COMMERCE_SALES_ENABLED`.
   - Set `COMMERCE_SIGNING_KEY` as a secret.
   - Set the Google service-account credentials and real-time developer
     notifications.
   - `deploy/gcp/deploy.sh` does not yet forward the commerce flags or the
     signing key; add them before deploying.
3. **Which books are paid**: `PUT /commerce/admin/titles/{slug}:{book|audio}`
   with metadata `{"premium": true}`. Leave `credits` at 0 to use the price for
   its length, or set your own.
4. **Build**: run an EAS build with `EXPO_PUBLIC_HYBRID_COMMERCE=true` and the
   ad variables in [ads-20261105.md](ads-20261105.md).
