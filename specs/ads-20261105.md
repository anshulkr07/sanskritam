# Ads for everyone, ad pages, player ads — 5–6 November 2026

Supersedes the opt-in parts of [admob-setup-20261104.md](admob-setup-20261104.md).
Owner's decisions:
- 2026-11-05: ads go between book pages as pages. Asking "Are you 18 or older?"
  would let everyone skip ads by answering no, so there is no age question.
- 2026-11-06: no audio ads. Google Ad Manager is not for this app. Instead, a
  normal (image or video) ad plays after some listening.
- 2026-11-06: no ad opens more of a paid book or audiobook. Previews are a fixed
  5% for everyone ([mudra-20261106.md](mudra-20261106.md)), so the "watch an ad
  for the next chapter" offer is gone.

## What the app does now

- **Who sees ads:** every free account, with no opt-in and no age question.
  - Every request is non-personalized and rated G (`maxAdContentRating`), so the
    same request suits any age and nothing is chosen from a minor's activity
    (DPDP Act s.9(3)).
  - Lite/Plus members and owners of an edition get none (server: `ad_policy.py`).
  - Settings → Advertising keeps Google's privacy-choices button (UMP) and says
    how to remove ads. There is no free off switch.
- **Ads launch without the Shop.** `EXPO_PUBLIC_ADS_MODE` no longer needs
  `EXPO_PUBLIC_HYBRID_COMMERCE`, and the backend mounts the ad routes on
  `COMMERCE_ADS_ENABLED` alone (`app/commerce/ads_router.py`). With purchases
  off, nobody is ad-free, and the ad routes need no commerce tables.
- **Book pages:** a full-page native ad is its own page in the page-turn book
  (`read/readerAds.native.ts`, `read/adRhythm.ts`, `components/SponsoredPage`).
  - When: one after every 8 verse pages and one before "Next chapter", at least
    3 minutes apart, none in a session's first 3 turns.
  - Where: two sheets ahead of the reader, and only once an ad has loaded, so a
    no-fill never leaves an empty page. Never between a painting and its verse.
  - The ad is drawn only while its page is the one being read, so a neighbour
    under the turning leaf never counts an impression. A video in the ad plays
    inside the ad's picture box and starts muted (Google's default, also
    requested here). Turning the page removes the ad and stops it.
  - Labelled "Advertisement" with Google's AdChoices icon; the media view is at
    least 120 dp.
  - The card that sat on the Next-chapter page is gone; the ad page replaces it.
- **Library card** (above the second shelf), a **Home card** (after the daily
  verse and its meaning) and a **player card** at the foot of the audiobook
  player's scrolling area. Native ads no longer need a server ticket.
- **Games** (`services/gameAds.ts`): a full-screen ad after every second
  finished game, at least 3 minutes apart, shown as the results sheet closes,
  never mid-game. It reuses the interstitial unit and loads while the results
  are read, so a no-fill never holds the player up.
- **Audiobook player ads** (`services/listenAds.ts`, `ChantController.holdFor`):
  - A full-screen interstitial (image or video) plays in the gap between two
    verses.
  - When: the first after half the interval, then once per interval of time
    actually playing. The interval is `listen_ad_minutes` from the server:
    `COMMERCE_LISTEN_AD_MINUTES`, default 10, never below 5. It waits up to
    3 minutes for a chapter end, then takes any verse gap.
  - Only while the player screen is in front and the app is open. Never over
    another screen, and never with the phone locked: AdMob serves no ads in the
    background, so screen-off listening has no ads.
  - The player shows "A short ad, then the chant continues" for 2.5 s first.
  - The chant waits silently. Next, Previous and Play wait, and the next verse
    plays when the ad closes; an ad that never closes gives way after 3 minutes.
  - The ad loads a minute before it is due. Without an interstitial unit in the
    build, there are no player ads.
- **Rewarded** no longer unlocks reading or listening (the server refuses the
  old `reading` and `audio` kinds). It now offers:
  - **Ask top-up**: when today's free questions are used up, "Watch a short ad
    to keep asking" raises that learner's own allowance for the day
    (`services/quota.ad_bonus`); the shared daily budget never moves. At most 3
    rewarded ads a day, and only while the shared budget has room.
  - **Practice time** on a paid book's verses outside its preview (`practice`).
  - A live build without a rewarded unit hides both.
- **Reward callback fixes:**
  - Google sends `ad_unit` as the number after the slash; the server now
    compares that, accepting either form in config.
  - Every callback Google signed gets HTTP 200, granting only when everything
    checks out, so AdMob's "Verify URL" test can pass. Only a bad signature
    gets 401.

1. **AdMob** (Android app):
   - **Native advanced** unit named "Book pages". Partner bidding: leave
     unchecked (it is only for apps whose mediation runs on another platform,
     such as AppLovin MAX). Media type: keep both Image and Video.
   - **Interstitial** unit named "Audiobook player", image and video.
   - Privacy & messaging → GDPR → create and publish a message.
   - Blocking controls → Sensitive categories → block what should not sit
     beside scripture.
   - Settings → Test devices → add your phone. Never tap your own ads.
   - **Rewarded** unit named "Ask top-up". Set its server-side verification URL
     to `https://shabd-api-829757572630.asia-south1.run.app/commerce/ads/ssv`
     (after this backend is deployed), list it in
     `backend/app/commerce/ad_config.json` (deploy.sh cannot pass lists), and
     set `EXPO_PUBLIC_ADMOB_ANDROID_REWARDED` for the build. Rewarded ads need
     `COMMERCE_ENABLED`, because the reward is recorded in the commerce tables.
   - The games reuse the "Audiobook player" interstitial unit; a separate unit
     only helps if you want their numbers apart in AdMob.
2. **Backend:**
   - Add `COMMERCE_ADS_ENABLED=true` to `.env.deploy` and deploy, backend
     before the app. `deploy/gcp/deploy.sh` now forwards it, and
     `COMMERCE_LISTEN_AD_MINUTES` if you want an interval other than 10.
   - The privacy page switches to the ad disclosure on that flag.
   - `COMMERCE_ADS_ENABLED=false` is the kill switch.
3. **Build:** a new native build is required; the App ID is compiled in, and an
   OTA update cannot add the SDK.
   - Set these on the EAS `production` environment (or in eas.json):
     `EXPO_PUBLIC_ADS_MODE=live`, `ADMOB_ANDROID_APP_ID=ca-app-pub-6672536899650972~…`,
     `EXPO_PUBLIC_ADMOB_ANDROID_NATIVE=ca-app-pub-6672536899650972/…`,
     `EXPO_PUBLIC_ADMOB_ANDROID_INTERSTITIAL=ca-app-pub-6672536899650972/…`.
   - iOS IDs are required only when building iOS.
   - The runtime becomes `<version>-ads-live-v1`. Publish later OTA updates with
     the same variables set, or they target the old runtime.
4. **Play Console:**
   - App content → Ads → Yes.
   - Advertising ID → used for advertising (live builds keep the AD_ID
     permission).
   - Data safety → device or other IDs shared with Google for advertising.
   - Keep the target audience 13+, as the privacy policy says the app is not for
     under-13s. An under-13 audience brings Play's Families rules.
   - Test on the internal track first.

## Audio ads: dropped

AdMob has no audio format; its six formats are banner, interstitial, rewarded
interstitial, rewarded, native and app open. Google's audio ads are Ad Manager
inventory. They need an AdSense account, then an Ad Manager account (Google
describes it as for large publishers), then linked audio demand, and Google's
IMA or PAL SDK in the app. The owner dropped this on 2026-11-06. The own-clip
audio breaks built on 2026-11-05 were removed the same day.

Sources read 2026-11-05/06:
