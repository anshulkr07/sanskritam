# Festival and deity artwork

Owner direction, 2026-10-14: include gods on the relevant festival pages. Do not restrict festival art to symbolic scenes. A sourced dhyana verse is optional context, not a prerequisite for generating, saving or displaying deity images.

Owner refinement, 2026-10-14: all deity images must use watercolor and gouache with visible paper texture and brushwork. Learn and Play may use other suitable styles. Keep figures dignified, anatomy coherent, faces recognizable, and traditional attributes clear. Avoid accidental lettering and watermarks. Check the rendered image against its intended arms, objects and mount.

Generate with the built-in ImageGen tool. Preserve full-resolution PNG masters in `results/artwork-2026-10-14/originals/`, optimized WebPs in `mobile/assets/festivals/`, and exact prompts and source paths in `specs/generated-art/manifest.json`. The manifest also preserves earlier symbolic scenes as reusable alternatives; deity-associated pages use the deity versions.

Lakshmi is used for Diwali, Durga and the nine Navadurga forms for Navaratri, Krishna for Janmashtami, Rama with Sita, Lakshmana and Hanuman for Rama Navami, Ganesha for Ganesh Chaturthi and related observances, Vishnu for Ekadashi, Shiva for Shivaratri and Pradosh, and Shani for Shani observances.

The mobile app bundles images locally for offline access. Uploaded server art remains available through the existing asset-management pipeline. Content and verse sourcing rules remain separate from artwork generation.
