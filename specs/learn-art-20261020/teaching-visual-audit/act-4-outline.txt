m1.weight [scene] Light and heavy
s1 story [IMAGE m1-weight-s1] The drum spirit
t1 teach Light and heavy | Every syllable is light ({{लघु}}) or heavy ({{गुरु}}). It is light when its vowel is short and nothing joined follows.
