Variant B

Manuscript — the poṭha, treated like one

The boldest of the three. The Read tab stops pretending to be an app store and becomes a library of editions: books stand on wooden shelves, contents pages read like a printed edition's front matter, the leaf you read is rubricated cream with gold hairlines, and the player sits in a deep maroon hall with gold type. Typography leads — a serif (Cormorant) joins the family for English display, Tiro carries all Sanskrit, ornaments replace icon-chrome.

  • Shelves are literal — wooden ledges under each rail
  • Contents as a printed table of contents, dot leaders and all
  • Leaf with margin rules, pada words underlined in gold, not boxed
  • Player a maroon hall — gold karaoke, gold transport
  • New English display face (Cormorant) — a real brand decision
