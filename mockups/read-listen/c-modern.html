Modern Reader — an audiobook app that happens to be Sanskrit

Pattern-matched to what people already know: Kindle for reading, a podcast app for listening. A bottom tab bar, a featured banner, snap-carousel Continue cards, chapter chips, a draggable bottom-sheet gloss, a floating control dock, a mini-player that follows you around, and a full-bleed Now Playing with a real timeline. Warm palette and deep fills stay — it still looks like Sanskritam.

  • Mini-player floats over everything until dismissed
  • Featured banner + Continue carousel on the shelf
  • Gloss arrives as a bottom sheet, not an inline block
  • Now Playing full-bleed cover, timeline, up next
  • Biggest structural change — new shell patterns to build
