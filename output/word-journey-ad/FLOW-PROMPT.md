## Accuracy and provenance

Sanskrit spelling: शर्करा / śarkarā. English sugar and German Zucker ultimately derive from it through intervening languages. They are separate borrowing paths, not a direct English-to-German chain.

- German: https://www.duden.de/rechtschreibung/Zucker
- English: https://www.etymonline.com/word/sugar
