Mudrā

Mudrā are Sanskritam's in-app coins. You get them by buying a Mudrā pack, and every plan month of Lite or Plus adds the Mudrā shown for that plan (an annual plan adds them one month at a time). Mudrā can be spent only inside Sanskritam, on books, audiobooks and Guru minutes, and later on other items the app offers for Mudrā. They have no cash value: they cannot be exchanged for money, cashed out, sold or transferred to another account. Mudrā you have do not expire, and stay in your account after a plan ends. Prices in Mudrā are the same in every country; the price of a pack depends on your country and is set by the store.

Before you spend Mudrā, the app shows the price and your balance. Something bought with Mudrā is yours to keep. If a store refunds a Mudrā pack or a plan payment, the Mudrā it added are removed, even if you have already spent them; your balance can then go below zero, and you can spend again once a later purchase brings it above zero. Things already bought stay yours.

Books and audiobooks

Each paid book has a free preview of 5% of its pages, and always at least one page. The preview pages are the same for every reader: the opening page, more from the early part of the book and a few from the middle and the end. Each paid audiobook plays its first 5% of running time free, and always at least its whole first verse. Buying a book or audiobook gives you a non-expiring licence to that edition, with offline access and background audio. It does not include future editions or AI conversations. Buying the book and its audiobook together costs less while you own neither. Lite and Plus members pay less, as described below; what they buy at that price stays theirs after the plan ends. Texts whose licences do not allow commercial use are free to read and save, and carry no ads.

Lite and Plus

Lite and Plus renew monthly or annually until cancelled in your store settings. A plan month starts on the day you subscribed or last renewed; an annual plan has twelve. Each plan month includes:

  • Lite: 150 Mudrā, 30 Guru minutes, 100 scored recitations, and 25% off books, audiobooks and premium avatars bought with Mudrā.
  • Plus: 500 Mudrā, 120 Guru minutes, 500 scored recitations, 50% off books and audiobooks bought with Mudrā, and every premium Guru avatar the app adds.

Both remove ads, save free books and chants for offline use while the plan lasts, and let your AI use go ahead even when the shared free allowance for the day is used up. Guru minutes and scored recitations included in a plan month do not carry over to the next month; Mudrā do. Without a plan you can score two recitations a day; competition entries are never counted. Plans do not by themselves open paid books. Cancelling stops renewal; the plan continues until the verified paid period ends, and your Mudrā and everything you bought stay. Google Play plan changes are scheduled at renewal; Apple shows when a change takes effect.

Guru voice

Each verified account can try Guru voice for three minutes once. Guru minutes bought with Mudrā do not expire and need no plan. The trial is used first, then the plan month's minutes, then bought time. A call lasts at most ten minutes; longer balances can be used across calls. Time starts when Guru's first spoken reply is sent and continues while the call stays connected, including listening and pauses. End the call to stop the timer. A failed start without a spoken reply uses no minutes, and unused reserved time returns to your balance. Voice availability depends on service readiness and capacity.

Use Restore purchases in the app. Manage subscriptions in your store; deleting the app or the account does not cancel store billing, and deleting the account removes your Mudrā and what you bought.
