Memberships, credits and title access

Purchases are processed by Google Play or Apple's App Store. The store displays your local price, billing period and renewal conditions. Monthly memberships renew automatically until cancelled in that store's subscription settings. Cancelling stops future renewals; paid benefits continue until the store-confirmed entitlement ends.

Credits purchased as refills or granted with a paid membership do not expire. Credits have no cash redemption or transfer value and are usable only inside Sanskritam. They are separate from experience points, competition results and referral rewards.

Every written book and audiobook requires its own credit unlock and occupies a separate slot. Non-members have five slots and online, foreground access for 365 days per unlock. Lite allows ten active titles, Plus twenty, and Pro has no active-title limit. Members receive offline licences and background audio while membership remains active.

Active membership extends existing unexpired title unlocks throughout membership. When paid membership ends, these titles retain 365 days of online access. A tier decrease or membership expiry does not remove existing unexpired unlocks, but a new unlock requires an available slot. Expired and surrendered titles require another purchase.

Surrendering an unlock permanently forfeits its remaining access without a credit refund. Non-member surrendered slots take seven days to become available. An optional 25-credit payment releases the slot immediately; the replacement title's price is charged separately. Members have no replacement cooldown.

Offline licences last up to seven days and never beyond the known paid entitlement. Connect to renew a licence. Downloads become unavailable after entitlement or licence expiry. Bookmarks and reading positions are kept separately.

Before paid AI work starts, the app displays the credit price or maximum reservation. Failed requests before useful delivery release reserved credits. AI voice charges accumulate in completed 15-second intervals at the accepted rate; unused reservations are released. Existing sessions retain their accepted rate.

Store refunds and chargebacks reverse the associated grant. If its credits were already spent, the account can have a credit deficit that must be resolved before further paid spending. Unrelated title unlocks remain intact. Follow your store's refund process; statutory consumer rights are unaffected.

Optional advertising and previews

Ads are limited and clearly marked. Rewarded ads are optional. Reading, audio and ordinary practice extensions last 24 hours, occupy no title slot and provide foreground access. Completion must be verified by the advertising provider. Advertising never improves a scored competition result or creates cash rewards. Manage advertising choices in Membership & credits.

Referrals and prizes

Eligible adult referrers accrue 20% of net subscription receipts from a referred customer's first three paid months. Refills, title purchases, supporter badges and hosting fees do not qualify. Rewards have a 45-day hold, a ₹500-equivalent payout threshold, refund adjustments, identity and duplicate-payment review, and country-specific fulfilment requirements. Purchased credits cannot become referral cash.

Competitions are free to enter. Their rules define eligibility, countries, judging, tie-breaks, prizes, appeals and delivery. Membership, credit purchases, ad viewing and referrals do not improve scoring or scored-attempt allowances. Apple and Google are not sponsors or administrators. Cash and real-world prize programmes are restricted to eligible adults.

Supporter badges and community hosting

Supporter badges are optional digital purchases, not charitable or tax-deductible donations. Organizer hosting is a separate product for an approved, free-entry event. Organizer fees and sponsor funds are recorded separately from prizes. Events require review of organizer identity, judging, moderation, funding and country fulfilment before publication. If purchased hosting cannot be delivered, contact support for the appropriate store refund process.

Commerce privacy

We store store-verified transaction identifiers, balances, spending records, title unlocks, account links and entitlement dates. Store credentials stay on the server. Referral delivery instructions are encrypted at rest and accessible to authorized finance administrators. Organizer recordings stay private for judging and are removed after the review retention period, normally 30 days after the event ends. Administrator financial actions are audited.

Deleting an account removes its personal reward profile, payout instructions, downloads and learning access. Pseudonymous purchase and accounting records may be retained for financial, fraud and legal obligations. Deleting the account does not cancel a store subscription: cancel it in your store settings before deleting the account. Contact support through the app for purchase, privacy or judging questions.
